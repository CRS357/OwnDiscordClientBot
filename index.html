Clear terms. Respectful handling of data.

This site contains the privacy policy and terms for the Discord Bot Client, a self-hosted web interface that connects to Discord through a bot account.

Before publishing: Replace every bracketed owner/contact/jurisdiction field in both policy documents with accurate information. These are editable policy templates, not legal advice. Do not publish them with placeholders or statements that do not match your actual deployment.

Read the policies

  • Privacy policy — explains the Discord data the app handles, its local files, external services, and retention.
  • Terms of service — explains acceptable use, Discord requirements, limitations, and contact details.

Publish with GitHub Pages

  1. Update the bracketed details and verify that the privacy descriptions match your hosting and bot configuration.
  2. Push this repository to GitHub.
  3. In the repository, open Settings → Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, select the branch containing these files and the /docs folder, then save.
  4. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish the site and open the URL shown in the Pages settings. Use that URL in your Discord Developer Portal application verification fields.

GitHub Pages hosts these static policy pages only. It does not host the bot, web client, or its runtime data.
